**If your monitor speakers feel flat, voices change first.** Speakers built into a laptop or monitor often let sound slip away or sound muffled. Put a bar speaker in front of the monitor and the sound comes straight at you, so voices in lectures or video calls sound much clearer.

Buyer Kim*A wrote, "The built-in monitor speakers felt stuffy, but voices sound clear now" (Oct 19, 2026, white). If you listen to long lectures, clearer voices alone make it less tiring.

**When you watch a film, the sound follows the picture.** The soundbar sits right under the monitor, so the sound feels like it comes from the screen. It fits well in front of a 24-27 inch monitor and takes little desk space.

Buyer Kang Dajim, who connected it to a TV, wrote, "The TV sound became clearer" (Oct 23, 2026, black). Films where sound builds the mood, like [the film The Matrix](https://aitechnotesen.blogspot.com/2026/11/the-matrix136-minutes-when-reality.html), make the difference easy to hear.

**At night, plug earphones into the front jack.** There is a headphone jack on the front, so at night you can plug in earphones and listen without waking anyone. Switching between the speaker and earphones is easy, so you can change with the time of day.

Kang Dajim also wrote, "The front volume and power controls are convenient" (Oct 23, 2026, black). Reviews also say earphones connect with almost no noise. At night, turn the dial down and it goes quiet right away.

## The downsides, too

```blog
type: image
path: images/03-img2.jpg
alt: White close-up — front volume dial and headphone and mic jacks
caption: White close-up — the front volume dial and headphone and mic jacks
width: 1200
align: center
url: https://link.coupang.com/a/iDz9nZgYiO
```

**The bass is weak, and the sound does not spread wide.** Britz says the premium drivers balance low and high tones (manufacturer claim). Even so, a bar without a separate woofer cannot deliver strong bass, and the sound does not fill the whole room.

Kim*A also listed it as a downside: "The left-right sound spread is weak" (Oct 19, 2026, white). If you mostly listen to bass-heavy music, a 2.1-channel speaker with a woofer is the right call. For listening at your desk, this size is enough.

**There is no Bluetooth.** It only connects by USB power and a wired 3.5mm cable. If you want to connect a phone or tablet wirelessly, this product cannot do it.

If wireless connection is a must, buy a Bluetooth speaker instead. If you only use it with a PC on your desk, the wired connection is more stable.

**A 1-star review says the jack and cable part is weak.** Buyer Chamchi To wrote, "The jack part bent after two days, and the replacement had the same problem a year later" (Apr 8, 2018, black). If you plug and unplug the cable often, this is worth a second thought.

Plug it in once and leave it, and the cable takes less strain. If you move it around often, look for a product with a fixed cable.
