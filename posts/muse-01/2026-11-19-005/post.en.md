**The tension screw spins free, so setting the height is fiddly.** Turn the screw with a driver alone and it free-spins, so getting the height right takes a while, according to several reviews. Plenty of buyers get stuck on this part at first setup.

Mount the monitor first, then turn the screw slowly with the hex key. Without the monitor's weight the arm can spring upward, so adjust it after the monitor is on.

**The joint just above the clamp feels a little loose.** The other joints are fine, but the joint right above the clamp turns slightly when you move the monitor, one review says. At first it even feels like a defect.

Tighten that joint once more with the hex key and it settles down. After setup, check each screw once more.

**The cables are not fully hidden.** This is not a cover that hides the cables inside the arm. The cables clip onto the arm, so some of the cable stays visible. If a perfectly clean cable run matters to you, this can disappoint.

Cable ties or a sleeve make it look much neater. It is not a functional problem, so pack some cable organizers with it.
