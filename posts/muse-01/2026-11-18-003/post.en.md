## How much does an e-ink photo frame cost?

The cheapest is the Waveshare ESP32-S3 PhotoPainter 7.3-inch.
It costs $105.58, with a 7.3-inch screen at 800×480.
It puts an e-ink screen in a wood frame, and it is the only one of the four below $110.

Next is the SwitchBot AI Art Frame 2 7.1-inch at $169.99.
Its screen is 7.1 inches at 1200×1600, which the spec sheet lists at 282 ppi, so photos look dense on a small screen.
The same line also has a 13.3-inch model at $399.99 and a 31.5-inch model at $1,399.99, so the price jumps sharply as the size grows.

Aura Ink comes in two sizes: the 10-inch at $349 and the 13.3-inch at $499.
Both share a 1600×1200 resolution, so the smaller 10-inch screen is denser at 200 ppi than the 13.3-inch at 150 ppi.
The price gap between the two is $150.

The four prices span more than 4 times from the cheapest to the most expensive.
Between $105.58 and $499, size and use decide the pick.
Price alone does not settle it, because the four work in different ways.

SwitchBot charges $3.99 a month separately for its AI art generation, so that feature adds to the cost.
The first 30 days are a free trial.
Aura Ink shares photos without a subscription, with no limit through its app.

## How do you use an e-ink photo frame?

With Aura Ink, you pick a photo in the app and send it over Wi-Fi.
You can change the photo up to 12 times a day, and one change takes about 30 seconds.
Aura claims up to three months of battery life when the photo changes once a day (maker claim).

With SwitchBot, you press a button on the back or tap the frame three times to change the picture.
A 16GB microSD card is included and holds about 5,000 images.
SwitchBot claims up to three years of battery life when the picture changes once a day (maker claim).

The Waveshare frame runs on USB-C power plugged in.
No battery is included, so you buy one separately, and a 16GB TF card comes in the box.
Its official documentation lists a full-screen change at about 25 seconds.

The Waveshare frame is closer to a device you set up with its official documentation than to a finished frame you use straight from the box.
If you want something easy from the start, SwitchBot or Aura Ink fits better.
Aura Ink has a front light that follows the room light, so photos stay visible in a dark room, and it turns off by itself at night.

## Who is an e-ink photo frame for?

A single photo on a desk points to the Waveshare frame or the SwitchBot 7.1-inch.
A picture on a living room wall points to the Aura Ink 13.3-inch.
In between, the Aura Ink 10-inch or the SwitchBot 13.3-inch fits.

Weight is worth checking too.
The Aura Ink 10-inch weighs 1.94 pounds and the 13.3-inch weighs 3.2 pounds.
Check how it mounts on a wall before you buy.

If you want to change photos often or play video, an e-ink frame is not the right pick.
Aura Ink does not play video or Live Photos, and one photo change takes about 30 seconds.
Changing photos many times a day also moves away from the battery claims.

The Aura Ink 13.3-inch was shown as sold out on the US official page in August 2026, according to a report.
