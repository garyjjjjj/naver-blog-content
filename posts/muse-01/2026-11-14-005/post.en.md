**Keys, wallet, bag — check where they are from your phone.** "Losing things was my everyday life. For me, this is a total lifesaver" (Lee*Young, 2026.11.02, 5 stars). It is small and light, so it slips into a wallet, onto keys, or into a bag, and once it is linked to the SmartThings app you can check the location right away. The minutes spent patting your pockets by the door get shorter.

**You can roughly tell where your child is.** "I bought it to check where my elementary-school kids are when they go to school or academy" (Lee*A, 2026.09.23, 5 stars). For places kids go every day, like school or academy, it worked fine as a rough location check. "It is light and easy to check the location, and I like it" (Seo*Hee, 2026.09.28) — tucked into a school bag, you barely feel the weight.

**The battery is barely something you think about. Up to 500 days, and you just swap it when it runs out.** It runs on a CR2032 coin battery, so there is nothing to charge. In power saving mode it lasts up to 700 days, according to Samsung. Clip it on once and leave it there for over a year.

## The downsides

**It is not real-time GPS, so a moving location does not show right away.** "If there is a downside, it is not real-time GPS, so you cannot see detailed movement routes" (Seo*Hee). "It does not keep showing my child's location in real time the way a phone does" (Lee*A). It shows the last confirmed location, so it does not suit tracking where something is second by second. It fits the "where did I put it" job — keys and bags.

**Some 1-star reviews say the location accuracy disappointed them.** "First, the location is not that accurate" (Jo*Jun, 2025.04.02, helpful to 49 people). "Location accuracy is very poor. In short, if you want it for my kind of use, I do not recommend it" (Seo*Ri, 2024.12.11). "The five-star and one-star reviews are so extreme, and in my case it is one star. It just does not do its job" (Kim Soo-yeon, 2026.04.14). Accuracy depends on how many Galaxy devices are nearby. In busy downtown areas and subways it locks on well; in quiet places you may only see the last location. If you buy it to check a child's location, know this limit before you buy.

**It works with Galaxy only. iPhone does not work.** You need the SmartThings app and a Samsung account, and it only works on Galaxy phones running Android 9.0 or later. The compass-style direction view needs a UWB Galaxy phone on Android 11 or later. iPhone users should look at the AirTag family instead.
