## The downsides, too

```blog

path: images/03-img2.jpg

```

**Some reviews say it feels too light to be stable.** It folds to palm size, which is good for carrying, but that also means less weight. Set a heavy tablet at a steep angle and it can feel less stable.

If you mostly leave it on your desk, the light weight is a plus — but if you stand a tablet on it often, check the weight first. A lower angle makes it much more stable.

**Some reviews say adjusting the height is stiff.** The hinge is stiff, so it holds its angle well, but you need some force to change it. Once you set the angle it stays put, so if you change angles often, your hand will feel it at first.

On the other hand, many reviews say it does not slip once set. If you settle on one angle you use often, the stiffness becomes a plus.

**Low-star complaints, too.** Some 1-star reviews say "the phone keeps slipping." There is a non-slip pad, but if your phone case is made of a slippery material, it may not grip well.

Take the case off, or set a lower angle, and the slipping gets better. If the case material is the problem, lowering the angle is the fastest fix.
