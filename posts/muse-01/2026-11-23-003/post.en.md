They are all called "AI that does the work for you," but some drive the browser directly while others turn research into documents.
This post checks the official pricing pages of all six on November 23, 2026, and puts price, free tiers, Korean support, and strengths into one table.

The first split is whether a free tier exists at all.
Operator and Mariner have no free tier, so you start on a paid plan from day one.
Manus, Genspark, and Perplexity can all be tried free.

Operator comes with the ChatGPT Pro plan.
The entry point is Pro 100 at $100 a month, with Pro 200 at $200 and Pro 500 at $500.
OpenAI does not publish message counts for Pro plans, so no figure like "400 messages a month" appears in this table.

Genspark charges $24.99 a month for Plus, $249.99 for Pro, and $40 per seat for Team.
Team credit figures differ between Genspark's own pages, so this table follows the Membership Plans page (10,000 credits per seat a month).

Three of the six can be used free.
Manus gives 300 credits a day, refreshed daily, and Genspark gives 100 credits a day.
Perplexity has a free plan of its own, built mainly around search.

But free tiers have ceilings.
Manus free credits work only on the Lite model and stop at 1,500 a month.
Genspark free credits also have a lifetime cap on how much you can ever receive.

Perplexity draws the free-paid line at features, not credits.
Its agent feature, Computer, starts with Pro ($20 a month), and Pro has no monthly credit allowance — it uses a weekly allowance instead.
The 10,000 monthly credits start with Max ($200 a month).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use an AI agent free?**

Manus, Genspark, and Perplexity can be used free. Manus gives 300 credits a day for the Lite model only, and Genspark gives 100 credits a day with a lifetime cap. Operator and Mariner have no free tier, so they start on paid plans.

**Why is there no price for Runner H?**

H Company has not published a consumer price. It sells mainly to companies and developers, and the only published price list is for the developer-facing Agents API, which is not a consumer price.

**What is the cheapest paid option?**

Manus Pro and Perplexity Pro both cost $20 a month. Manus includes 4,000 credits a month, while Perplexity Pro has no monthly credits and uses a weekly allowance for Computer instead. The same $20 works differently, so check whether you need credits first.
