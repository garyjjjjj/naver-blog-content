**Your neck feels better even after long hours.** When the screen comes up to eye level, you spend less time looking down. A laptop screen below your eyes leaves your neck stiff after a few hours, and a stand changes that posture.

One buyer wrote that working on a laptop on this stand left their back and shoulders feeling fine. If you sit at a desk for long hours, screen height is the first thing to fix.

**Heavy laptops do not sag.** The 2-axis hinge locks firmly, so the stand does not sink even when you type with the laptop on it. Reviews keep coming back to how strong the hold is.

Like [automating Instagram card news with Claude Code](https://aitechnotesen.blogspot.com/2026/11/automating-instagram-card-news-with.html), changing one desk tool at a time eventually changes how you work. Once the screen height is right, where the external keyboard goes falls into place too.

**Cooling holes help when the work runs hot.** The holes in the base let heat escape from the bottom of the laptop. They are ventilation holes, not a fan, so they do not cool the laptop directly.

If you often edit video or play games that heat the laptop up, a metal base with cooling holes is a better bet than a plastic one. There is no fan, so there is no noise or power cable to worry about.

**The hinge is stiff, so the first adjustment takes force.** The strong hold comes at a price: setting the height and angle the first time takes both hands. One review says even an adult man found it stiff.

Press the base down on a flat surface and pull slowly with both hands to adjust it. Once you set an angle it stays put, so if you rarely change angles, the stiffness turns into a plus.

**Typing hard on the built-in keyboard makes the screen wobble a little.** One review says the screen wobbled slightly when typing hard with a 14-inch LG Gram on the stand. The laptop is bigger than the stand, so the bounce comes from that. The stand itself does not sink.

Use it with an external keyboard and the wobble mostly goes away. If you only ever type on the laptop's own keyboard, this is worth knowing before you buy.

**Some reviews say the stand is not level.** One buyer found the left side sat slightly lower than the right. It did not get in the way of using it, but it can bother you if you are sensitive to that sort of thing.

Check the level as soon as it arrives, and ask for an exchange if it looks off. The rubber pads hold the laptop in place, so a slight tilt does not stop you from using it.
