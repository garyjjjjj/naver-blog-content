## What Are the AirPods Pro 3?

The AirPods Pro 3 are wireless earbuds Apple announced on September 9, 2025. **They went on sale on September 19, and Apple Korea lists them at 369,000 won.** The outside looks much like the Pro 2: short stems, a white case, and silicone ear tips.

The changes are inside. **Noise cancelling got stronger, and a heart rate sensor and live translation are new.** The ear tips use a foam-infused material now and come in five sizes. The case earned a dust and water rating too. The H2 chip and Bluetooth 5.3 carry over unchanged.

## How Much Stronger Is the Noise Cancelling?

Apple says the Pro 3 cancel up to twice as much noise as the Pro 2. **Independent testing backs up the gap.** In SoundGuys measurements, average noise reduction was 90% for the Pro 3 and 83% for the Pro 2. RTINGS also found the Pro 3 isolate better against street noise and higher-frequency sounds.

Your commute is the first test for switching. **If subway and bus noise reach your ears two hours a day, the difference shows up before the price does.** If you only use earbuds in a quiet office and at home, ask first whether the Pro 2 ever felt short on noise blocking. If it never did, the new features are not worth the money.

The ear tips matter as much as the sensor. **You need the right size from the five foam-infused tips for the noise cancelling to work properly.** A poor seal lets sound leak in no matter how good the sensor is. After buying the Pro 3, run the ear tip fit test first.

## Who Gets Use Out of Heart Rate Sensing and Live Translation?

Heart rate sensing speaks first to people who work out. **A sensor in the earbuds measures heart rate and calories while you run, and shows them in the iPhone Fitness app.** It covers 50 workout types, and the record stays with just the earbuds and an iPhone, no watch needed. With heart rate on, battery life drops to 6.5 hours, and an iPhone on iOS 26 or later is required.

Live translation helps on trips. **It plays the other person's words back in your ears translated, and shows your own words on the iPhone screen in their language.** Korean is included, and the feature still carries a beta label. It needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence on iOS 26, running on iPhone 15 Pro or newer.

Both features need an iPhone. **On an Android phone the Pro 3 pair as plain Bluetooth earbuds, and translation and heart rate sensing drop out.** If you do not use an iPhone, half of what is new in the Pro 3 goes missing. Galaxy phone owners are better off matching an earbud to their own phone at the same price.

## Is There Anything the Pro 2 Does Better?

Total battery life with the case is longer on the Pro 2. **The Pro 3 last 8 hours per charge, but the total with the case is up to 24 hours.** The Pro 2 give 6 hours per charge and up to 30 hours with the case. On a trip where you cannot charge for two days, the Pro 2 total is more reassuring.

Hearing features are split between the two. **The hearing test and hearing aid features carry over to the Pro 3 unchanged.** The hearing aid with automatic Conversation Boost and Hearing Protection are not supported in Korea, though. If you bought the Pro 2 for its hearing features, the range you can use in Korea stays the same after switching.

New Pro 2 stock is also getting hard to find. **Apple's store moved on to the Pro 3, so remaining new Pro 2 pairs come from retailer stock.** In the United States, the Pro 3 sold for $199 on Amazon on November 11, while the only new Pro 2 listing was $249 from a third-party seller. Refurbished pairs run from $144 to $174. If you find a cheap Pro 2 in Korea, check the warranty and battery condition first.

## Price and Buying from Korea

The Pro 3 sell for 369,000 won at the Apple Store, with no discount. **On Amazon in the United States, they fell to $179 during Black Friday week on November 21.** That is a US price, so do not mistake it for a Korean buying price. The Pro 2 launched in Korea at 359,000 won, so the list-price gap is 10,000 won.

Official sales in Korea run through the Apple Store and authorized resellers. **There is no separate direct-import condition.** When a discount seller offers the Pro 3, check that it is genuine and what the warranty covers. Five ear tip sizes ship in the box, so plan on finding the right fit after purchase.

## My Take

If this is your first pair, the Pro 3 are the answer. For a 10,000-won list-price gap you get stronger noise cancelling, heart rate sensing, and translation at once. If you already own the Pro 2, switch only when commute noise or workout tracking left you wanting. If neither did, keeping the 369,000 won is the better call.

- ✅ If you use an iPhone and are buying earbuds for the first time, buy the Pro 3
- ✅ If commute noise is loud, or you want heart rate tracking while working out, switch
- ⚠️ If the Pro 2 battery and noise blocking still satisfy you, keep them
- ⚠️ If you use an Android phone, check first that translation and heart rate sensing drop out

For Pro 2 owners, the Pro 3 earn their price only when noise or workouts fall short, and for first-time iPhone buyers, 369,000 won is a fair choice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do they work with an Android phone?**

They pair over Bluetooth and play music. Live translation, heart rate sensing, and automatic device switching need an iPhone, and Apple notes that features may be limited on Android.

**Do the hearing test and hearing aid work in Korea?**

Yes. The hearing test and hearing aid features that arrived with the Pro 2 carry over to the Pro 3. The hearing aid with automatic Conversation Boost and Hearing Protection are not supported in Korea, though.

**Is it still worth buying a cheap Pro 2 now?**

In the United States, refurbished pairs run from $144 to $174. Apple no longer sells the Pro 2 in Korea, so check the price, warranty, and battery condition on any remaining stock first.
