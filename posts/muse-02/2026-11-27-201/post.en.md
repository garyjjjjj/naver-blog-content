## What Is the TinyPod?

It is a case for the Apple Watch. Take the strap off, put the watch in, and you get a small device that looks like an iPod. A round scroll wheel sits below the screen. It was announced on July 17, 2024, and it is still on sale. **The case itself has no battery, no Bluetooth, and no cellular radio.** Calls, messages, music, and payments all come from the watch inside. It comes in three sizes, 40/41 mm, 44/45 mm, and 49 mm, and fits Series 4 through 9, SE, and Ultra generations 1 through 3. **Series 10 and 11 are not on the official compatibility list.**

## Does the Wheel Really Work Like an iPod?

Scrolling works, but it is not the iPod click wheel. **Turn the wheel and a rubber pulley inside physically rotates the Digital Crown.** It is purely mechanical, so it uses no battery and needs no Bluetooth connection. Pocket-lint reports that the watch's haptics vibrate through the case as you scroll, which gives it some feel.

The center button is fixed plastic and does not press down. **You have to tap the screen or use the side button to select anything.** A Laptop Mag reviewer wrote that turning it with two fingers, like a tiny steering wheel, works better than one finger. Reports of the wheel slipping for lack of friction keep coming up. A Fly Through Wires reviewer wrote that the wheel did not turn the crown at all, and tried two different watches in the case. That points to manufacturing variance.

## What Changes When You Leave Your Phone Behind?

A cellular watch is the precondition. With a cellular model and a plan, you can call, message, listen to music, and pay without your phone. **With a GPS-only watch, it is closer to an offline music player in your pocket.** The Verge put it this way: maybe the dumbphone of our dreams was just a smartwatch all along.

The maker says turning wrist detection off stretches the battery to several days. User reports disagree. Some Reddit reports put standalone cellular use at 5 to 10 hours. **With wrist detection off, Apple Pay asks for a passcode every time, and a lost watch stays unlocked.** Health tracking also stops while the watch is off your wrist. Continuous heart-rate and sleep tracking stop, and ECG is impossible because the crown is covered.

The finish deserves lowered expectations too. Fly Through Wires described oily-looking white plastic with a visible gap between the front and back shells. **The verdict was that it feels more like a toy than a $79 case.**

## Price and Buying in Korea

The US price starts at $79. **At 1,343.35 won per dollar (November 26, 2026), that converts to about 106,000 won.** The Lite without the wheel is $29, about 39,000 won. Shipping and import duties are extra, so the real cost is higher. The launch markets were the US, Australia, Canada, France, Germany, and the UK. **Direct shipping to Korea is unconfirmed on the official site.** There is no official Korean seller either, so check the official store for Korea shipping before ordering.

If you do not own a watch, add the watch to the case price. Repurposing a spare Series 8 or SE is the most sensible use.

| Pick | Key figure | Choose it when |
|---|---|---|
| TinyPod | $79, wheel included, hard case | You want the wheel feel and have a spare watch |
| TinyPod Lite | $29, no wheel, soft case | You only want the pocketable feel and not the wheel |
| Apple Watch as-is | $0, wrist health tracking stays | You need a daily watch and health features first |

## Check Before You Buy

- ✅ You have a spare Apple Watch (Series 4 to 9, SE, or Ultra) and it is a cellular model
- ✅ You want to leave your phone behind for walks, workouts, and errands
- ✅ iPod nostalgia and the wheel feel are the point
- ⚠️ Series 10 or 11 will not fit (not on the official compatibility list)
- ⚠️ A GPS-only watch works only as a music player
- ⚠️ You cannot give up wrist detection and health tracking

## My Take

TinyPod is closer to a toy than a practical product. **Still, if you have a spare Apple Watch and a cellular plan, $79 buys a day out without your phone.** Lower your expectations for the wheel and the finish, and the $29 Lite is a fair way to try the idea. The call is simple. If you have a spare watch, want to leave your phone behind, and can set health tracking aside for a while, it is worth buying.

Check three things before ordering: a spare watch, a cellular model, and not a Series 10 or 11.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do Series 10 and 11 watches really not fit?**

The official compatibility list covers only Series 4 through 9, SE, and Ultra generations 1 through 3. Series 10 and 11 have different body sizes, so they most likely do not fit. Check the size selector on the official store before ordering.

**Is it worth buying if I do not own an Apple Watch?**

No. The case alone does nothing. If you buy a watch just to put in it, the case price plus the watch price may make a used iPhone SE or a budget smartphone the better buy.
