A mug that shows pictures on a screen — the PixelMug P1

![A 4.83-inch pixel LED screen hidden under the porcelain surface — it lights up when switched on (illustration)](images/01-hero.jpg)

You've looked at your mug while pouring your morning coffee, right?
What if that cup had a small screen in it and the pictures moved?
Today's product is a smart mug with a pixel display hidden in a porcelain body.
This introduction is based on published materials.

## What is this mug?

![The screen is 32×16 pixels, and a temperature sensor shows your drink's temperature in real time (illustration)](images/02-screen.jpg)

The PixelMug P1 is a mug with a 4.83-inch full-color pixel LED screen set into a porcelain body.
The screen is 32 pixels wide by 16 pixels tall, hidden under the porcelain surface until it lights up when switched on.
With the dedicated app (BUBBLE) you can upload pixel art or animations you drew yourself, and AI can also turn a photo into a pixel portrait.
A built-in temperature sensor shows your drink's temperature on the screen in real time.

## Why it's fun

![You can upload pixel art and turn photos into AI portraits with the dedicated BUBBLE app (illustration)](images/03-app.jpg)

**First, the cup becomes a message board.**
Type a phrase in the app and it appears on the screen like scrolling subtitles.
Things like "In a meeting" or "How about a cup of coffee?"
You can also display a QR code on the screen, so it can stand in for a business card.

**Second, AI draws the pictures for you.**
Upload a selfie and it turns into an 8-bit pixel portrait in a few seconds.
Enter a prompt and AI generates new pixel art.
Even if you can't draw, you can use a cup with a different design every day.

**Third, it's a cup that's also a clock and a weather notifier.**
It can show pixel clocks in several styles, and weather information is displayed too.
Mini-games (a falling ball, a slot machine and more) are built in to enjoy on a break.
It also has a gyroscope, so there are interactions that react when you tilt it.

## The catch

The battery is 600mAh, so it lasts only about 2 hours of continuous use.
It's better suited to short bursts of enjoyment than to keeping the screen on all day.
At 175ml, the capacity is smaller than a regular mug, so it suits espresso or small drinks.
It can't go in a microwave or dishwasher — hand washing only.
At 350g, it's also on the heavy side for an empty cup.

## Editor's take

It suits someone who wants it on their desk, especially anyone into desk decor.
It also looks fun as a gift. Using a cup with a different picture every day is definitely a new experience.
But for anyone looking for a practical mug, the battery and capacity may disappoint.
Think of it as buying "fun" for about $110 and it makes sense.
