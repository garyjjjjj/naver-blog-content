A mouse usually hides under your palm. The Lenovo 900 transparent wireless mouse turns that around: a clear shell over a mirror-like inner body makes it something worth leaving on the desk even when idle. This post is based on published materials, not a hands-on review.

![A transparent wireless mouse on a tidy desk (illustration)](images/01-hero.jpg)

Transparent, yet no circuits on show — the trick is the mirrored inner body. (Illustration)

> Key takeaway
>
> ### **The fun of a transparent mouse is not the circuits. It is the shining inner body.**

## What is different?

The outside is a clear shell and the inside is a mirror-polished housing. Unlike typical transparent gadgets, it does not exhibit chips and wires. Light reflects between the two layers so the inner body seems to float, according to Yanko Design (article dated 2026-10-01, checked 2026-10-27). The body is a symmetrical pebble shape weighing about 125g — heavier than a light travel mouse. The split buttons are aluminum alloy, the scroll wheel is metal, and button life is rated at about 5 million clicks (maker guidance, checked 2026-10-27). Independent verification of scratch and yellowing resistance for the clear shell was not found.

## One mouse for three devices

Connectivity covers 2.4GHz wireless and two Bluetooth channels, so up to three devices can be registered and switched. Moving from a laptop to a tablet needs no mouse swap. Supported systems are Windows, macOS, Android, Linux, and ChromeOS (checked 2026-10-27). The PixArt PAW3222 optical sensor adjusts from 400 to 4,000 DPI — fast across wide screens, slow for detailed work.

![A transparent mouse used between a laptop and a tablet (illustration)](images/02-multi.jpg)

Switching devices takes one button. (Illustration)

## Battery, price, and the China-launch condition

The built-in battery is 300mAh. The maker estimates about 90 days on an eight-hour daily use basis, with a full USB-C charge taking about two hours (checked 2026-10-27). The China launch price is 399 yuan (about $59) (checked 2026-10-27). Sales, pricing, and shipping outside China are unconfirmed, as is Korean use of the built-in Tianxi AI assistant shortcut.

## Who is it for?

It fits people who keep a tidy desk but want one object to stand out, and people who work across several devices and value the three-device switching. If a light travel mouse matters most, 125g may feel heavy, and since Korean sales are unconfirmed, the buying route needs checking first.
