## What Is This Mouse?

The myAir.0 OriMouse is a folding Bluetooth mouse designed by Hong Kong designer Horace Lam. Yanko Design's store sells it under the name OrigamiSwift. Folded, it lies flat like a card; unfolded, magnets snap it into a triangular, full-size mouse.

**Its starting point is designing the working shape and the storage shape separately.** In use it measures 4.53 x 2.83 x 1.65 inches, close to a regular mouse, and folded it shrinks to 4.8 x 4.96 x 0.18 inches. The sensor area is 0.39 inches (10mm), slightly thicker. It weighs 40g.

It began as the Air.0 on Kickstarter in 2022 and **now sells as a mass-produced product on the official site.** According to the maker, it made TIME's Best Inventions of 2026 list and won a 2026 IDEA Silver Award. Buyer ratings on the official site stand at 4.7 out of 5 from 365 reviews.

## What Changes When It Folds?

Thickness decides how much room a mouse takes in a bag. Folded, the OriMouse slips into a laptop sleeve or a shallow drawer, and the maker says it springs open in under 0.5 seconds.

**This mouse saves bag volume, not bag weight.** At 40g it is light, but its real value is going flat. In cafes or shared offices, where a workspace appears and disappears every day, one less object sits on the desk.

It connects over Bluetooth 5.2 with no dongle. It charges over USB-C, and the maker claims up to three months per charge. Treat that figure as a manufacturer claim until independent testing confirms it. Macworld noted that using it while charging tips the light body toward the cable side.

Macworld's review recommends a mouse pad. On a shiny desk the cursor stuttered; on a pad it moved fast and accurately. A dedicated BagPad (a bag that doubles as a mouse pad, $19) is sold separately, though a flat book or magazine works too.

## How Do You Scroll?

There is no wheel. A touch scroll pad sits on the front slope, and you scroll by drawing a short line with a finger. The center button only works when pressed and held for 1 to 2 seconds.

**Hands used to a wheel need time to adjust.** Macworld's reviewer wrote that it might feel natural after a few weeks, but switching straight from a wheel mouse demands patience. Buyer reviews on the official site repeat the same point about the touch sensor taking time to learn.

**There is no horizontal scroll either.** If you often sweep sideways across spreadsheets, this will bother you first. The left and right buttons are mechanical, with a click feel close to a regular mouse. The claim that lower buttons ease wrist strain is the maker's, so the effect will vary by person.

## Compared With Other Mice

Three mice Macworld measured side by side show where the OriMouse stands.

| Mouse | Key figures | Pick it when |
|---|---|---|
| OriMouse | Folds flat, 40g, 4,000dpi, up to 3 months | You want to shrink bag volume |
| Apple Magic Mouse | 1,300dpi, about 1 month | You want Mac gestures as they are |
| Logitech MX Master 4 | 8,000dpi, 70 days | You do long precise work at a desk |

The figures come from Macworld's review. **Folding and portability are the only categories the OriMouse wins.** Desk mice lead on precision, battery, and gestures. Only buy the OriMouse from this table if you need a mouse that folds.

## What to Check Before Buying

Hand size comes first. Even though its in-use size is close to a regular mouse, the triangular grip feels different from hand to hand. Official reviews include a note wishing it were slightly larger. If your hands are large, measure the real size (11.5 x 7.2cm) against your palm first.

**The folding mechanism's durability is still unverified.** The maker cites a patented triangular structure, but no long-term data on thousands of folds is public. Macworld did note that its light weight and structure help it survive drops better than a regular mouse.

Watch out for fakes too. An independent review warns about counterfeit sites and eBay sellers and advises buying directly from the official site. Yanko Design's store (YD Select) is also an authorized seller.

## My Take

If you carry a laptop bag every day and the bulk of a mouse bothers you, this one is worth a look. **The $85 buys the fold itself.**

- ✅ Mobile work: when your desk appears and disappears in cafes and shared offices
- ✅ A lighter bag: when you want the mouse to take less room
- ✅ Check first: hand size, adjusting to touch scrolling, and Korea shipping costs

If a mouse already sits fixed on your desk, there is little reason to buy one. A mouse that never folds is just a mouse.
