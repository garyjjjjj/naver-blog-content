8BitDo Forest Wonder Retro 87: An Xbox Keyboard That Does Not Work on Xbox

Buying a keyboard with a bright Xbox logo, only to find it does not plug into a console, stings more than the price. The 8BitDo Forest Wonder Retro 87 is an officially licensed Xbox product, but it does not work with Xbox consoles. It is a mechanical keyboard and a wireless mouse for PC and Android, sold separately. This post pulls together 8BitDo's official materials, independent reviews, and press coverage from Korea and abroad.

## What Are This Keyboard and Mouse?

Forest Wonder is the keyboard and mouse from 8BitDo's Cozy Collection, made with Xbox. A pixel-art forest runs across the keycaps and the case. It launched in the United States on September 1, 2026, and reached the UK and Europe on November 11, 2026.

**The keyboard and mouse are not a set.** The keyboard costs $119.99 and the mouse costs $59.99, each sold on its own. Together they come to $179.98. If you already have a mouse you like, you can buy only the keyboard.

## What Changes When a Pixel Forest Moves Onto Your Desk?

**The forest is not printed on the surface, it is dyed into the keycaps.** Dye sublimation puts the pixel animals inside the PBT plastic, so they do not wear off with use. The RGB backlight switches between eight modes, which 8BitDo compares to fireflies in a quiet forest.

The switches are Core Green tactile switches, co-developed with Kailh. **The PCB is hot-swappable, so you can swap switches without soldering.** When your taste changes, change the switches, not the board. A dial on the left of the keyboard switches connection modes and controls volume.

**The Dual Super Buttons are two large buttons that sit beside the keyboard.** You can assign macros to them and remap them on the hardware, without opening any software. They weigh 270g, so they stay put on the desk.

The battery is 4,000mAh. **It lasts up to 280 hours over 2.4G wireless and up to 300 hours over Bluetooth.** Charging takes 4 to 8 hours. These are manufacturer measurements, so real battery life varies with your settings.

## Why Does the Mouse Come With a Dock?

The Retro R8 is a 77g wireless mouse. **It pairs a PAW 3395 sensor with up to 26,000 DPI and 8,000Hz polling over a wired connection.** Over 2.4G it reaches up to 4,000Hz, and over Bluetooth it drops to 125Hz. The 450mAh battery lasts up to 100 hours over Bluetooth, and 26 to 105 hours over 2.4G depending on the polling rate.

**The charging dock is more than a stand.** It holds the 2.4G adapter inside and works as a signal extender, so the connection holds even when the PC sits under the desk. A left-hand mode is included, so left-handed users are covered too.

## It Has an Xbox Logo, So Why Won't It Plug Into a Console?

**The official FAQ answers "No."** The spec sheet lists only Windows 10 (1903 and later) and Android 9.0 and later as supported. Xbox Series X and S are not on the list. The license covers the design and the button, not a console connection.

Pressing the Xbox button does not turn on a console. **The button opens Game Bar (Win+G) on Windows.** The official FAQ says it cannot be remapped. For anyone shopping for a console keyboard, that one line settles it.

**The keyboard layout needs a check too.** Only a US (ANSI) layout is sold, with no Korean legends. If you are used to a Korean layout, expect an adjustment period. macOS is not on the official support list either, so it works only as a basic Bluetooth keyboard there.

If lighting controls stop working on Windows 11, the official guidance is to set the lighting mode to 8BitDo RGB in Ultimate Software V2 and turn off Windows Dynamic Lighting.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does it type on an Xbox if I plug it in with a cable?**

The official FAQ says "No." Some reviews report that basic typing works over a cable, but the dials, RGB lighting, and button functions do not work on a console, so do not buy it as a console accessory.

**Can I remap the Xbox button to something else?**

No. The official FAQ says the button cannot be remapped, and on Windows it only opens Game Bar (Win+G).
