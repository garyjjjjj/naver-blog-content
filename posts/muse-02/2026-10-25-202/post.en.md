Stop digging through your bag — MagQuick, a strap that snaps on and pulls off

Have you ever dug through your bag looking for your phone when you wanted to take it out? "MagQuick" from Japan's Active is a quick-release strap that hangs a phone, keys, or a light from a bag or belt on a magnetic buckle. Bring the buckles close and they snap together; pull and they come apart — so the things you reach for stay within reach. It is on Japan's crowdfunding platform Makuake until November 15, 2026. This is a write-up based on published materials, not a hands-on review.

![A quick-release strap on a backpack shoulder strap — a smartphone holder connected by a magnetic buckle (illustration)](images/01-hero.jpg)

## What is this strap?

The product is called "MagQuick." It uses the magnetic "V-Buckle" from Germany's FIDLOCK. Bring the buckle halves together and magnets pull them in; a mechanical lock then engages so the hold does not rely on magnets alone. Pull to release when you need the item. It weighs 35g, is made of aluminum alloy and nylon, comes in camouflage and black, and includes a smartphone strap holder.

## Why is it interesting?

**First, getting things out gets shorter.** Hang the phone from a backpack shoulder strap and both hands stay free while moving; pull it off for a photo, a payment, or a ticket gate. The official description notes that because the strap stays connected while in use, it also helps stop a slip from becoming a drop.

**Second, it hangs anywhere.** A backpack shoulder strap, a bag, a belt, or a belt loop — the same strap works for commuting, travel, and the outdoors. Action cameras, lights, multitools, card cases, and earphone cases are all listed as things it can carry.

**Third, the buckle itself is a trusted part.** A rated load of about 15kg covers a phone, keys, and a small light with room to spare. The official page is explicit, though, that the FIDLOCK part is only part of the product — the whole strap is not German-made.

## Check before buying

This is a crowdfunding product, and that has to be accepted first. Makuake's all-in format means the purchase stands once you pay by November 15, 2026, whether or not the funding goal is met. Delivery of rewards is planned for late December 2026 but can slip if support exceeds expectations. Cancellation for a delay is generally not allowed; only if delivery passes three months beyond the planned month can supporters request cancellation. As a product still in development, the design, specs, and colors may partly change, and shipping to Korea is unconfirmed. Listed prices include Japanese tax and domestic shipping.
