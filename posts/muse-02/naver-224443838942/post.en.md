A macro pad with a joystick — ComboX from a Korean startup

![A macro console packing 10 mechanical keys, a rotary dial, a joystick and an LCD into one body (illustration)](images/01-hero.jpg)

Repeat the same work every day and your fingers tire first.
Copying and pasting, switching between apps, pressing the same shortcut ten times.
The product introduced today started from the idea of handing that repetitive work to a small machine on your desk.
This introduction is based on published materials.

## What is this device?

![The key switches are hot-swappable — replaceable without tools (illustration)](images/02-controls.jpg)

ComboX is a macro console made by the Korean startup Electrocookie.
It looks like a keyboard, but it isn't one.
It's a desktop controller that packs 10 mechanical keys, a rotary dial, a joystick, and a small LCD screen into one body.
It is currently being funded on Kickstarter, with shipping scheduled for December 2026.

> **One line: a desktop control stick that bundles the tasks you use often into a single physical button.**

## Why it's fun

**First, you move between virtual desktops with the joystick.**
You can move across Windows virtual desktops or macOS Spaces with the joystick.
It's a way to switch screens with one finger, without memorizing keyboard shortcuts.
For people who move between many tasks, this looks like a point that would become comfortable once it gets into your hand.

![AI Link sends a captured screen straight to an AI service together with a saved prompt (illustration)](images/03-ailink.jpg)

**Second, it connects a screen capture straight to AI.**
According to the maker, the "AI Link" feature can send content captured from the screen straight to an AI service together with a saved prompt.
The goal is to cut down the process of capturing, opening an AI service, pasting, and typing a prompt.
How smoothly it actually works could not be confirmed, as there are no independent reviews yet.

**Third, it stores different layouts for each app across 7 layers.**
You can keep separate key settings by purpose, such as for video editing, streaming, or document work.
The key switches are said to be hot-swappable, replaceable without tools.

![Different key layouts for each app, stored across 7 layers (illustration)](images/04-usage.jpg)

## There are downsides too

First, note that this is a crowdfunding product.
Since it's funded on Kickstarter, it's wise to keep the possibility of shipping delays in mind.
The exact price was not disclosed in the press release, either.
Only early-bird pricing is said to apply, so the actual amount needs to be checked directly on the Kickstarter campaign page. (Verified October 16, 2026)
You'll also likely need time to get used to setting up the dedicated software.

## Editor's take

It looks suited to people who do video editing, streaming, and work that moves between several AI tools.
With Premiere Pro, After Effects, and OBS integration highlighted, creators look like the main target.
On the other hand, for people who only use a few shortcuts, a regular macro pad may be enough.
The deciding factor will likely be whether the feel of this device is appealing enough to accept the crowdfunding risk.
