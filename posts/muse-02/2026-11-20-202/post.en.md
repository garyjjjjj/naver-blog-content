FansDreams Folio: A Book-Sized Folding Desk, 3 Things to Check Before Backing

## What Is Folio?

FansDreams Folio is a folding portable desk from iSwift. The FansDreams brand says Folio grew out of feedback from users of its earlier product, the iSwift Pi portable desk. **It moves a desk from a board you unfold to a board you fold away.**

Folded, it is the size of an A5 book and 30mm thick. Open, it becomes a raised work surface. It works in four forms: mouse pad, laptop stand, lap desk, and side table. The campaign says setup and folding take 3 seconds.

The Standard opens to 440mm wide and weighs 1.23kg. Folded, it is A5-sized and 30mm thick. The Max opens to 540mm wide and weighs 1.53kg. **That weight matches a light laptop.** It fits a car trunk or a move around the house better than a daily bag.

## What Makes It Interesting?

The fun is that four forms come from one board. The mouse pad works where a desk surface fights the mouse, and the laptop stand lifts the screen toward eye level. The lap desk becomes a table on your knees on a bed or sofa, and the side table becomes a small surface next to a bed.

**One board doing four jobs is where this desk starts.** You stop hunting for a new surface every time you move. The same board folds and opens again from the sofa to the bed.

The materials match the folding idea. The outside is PU leather, the inside is fiberglass composite panels with a dense fiberboard core. The maker says it is reinforced with 0.8mm galvanized steel plate and folds on SK7 steel and stainless steel hinges. It looks like a book outside and is built sturdily inside.

## Where Does It Wobble?

The 8kg load rating is a maker claim. The maker also says the hinge passed an internal test of 100,000 open-and-close cycles. Neither figure has independent verification yet. **Treat the numbers as reference and watch real-use footage first.**

The maker itself explains that the composite panels have some flex, bending slightly under load and recovering after. Supporting weight and staying steady while typing are different questions. A hand on one edge or weight on one side can make the board feel like it tilts. That matters first if you type for hours on it.

Size needs the same hard look. The Standard top is 440mm wide, room for a laptop and a mouse, but too narrow for a keyboard and papers side by side. **Forget it is a temporary desk and you will be disappointed.** It does not replace a desk you sit at all day.

## Standard or Max: How to Choose

| Option | Key figure | Choose it when |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 440mm wide, 1.23kg, up to 14-inch laptops | You carry it in a bag and use it on a sofa or bed |
| Max | 540mm wide, 1.53kg, over 14-inch laptops | You have a larger-screen laptop and want a roomier top |

The maker fits the Standard to laptops up to 14 inches and the Max to laptops over 14 inches. **Do not choose by screen diagonal alone.** Measure the laptop's real footprint, cable positions, and room for your hands. The depth is nearly the same on both models, 240mm and 245mm, so the Max gets wider without getting deeper.

## My Take

If you often need a temporary work surface moving between bed, sofa, and car, it is worth a look. **What you pay for is a desk that folds away.**

- ✅ Temporary desk: when you often need a surface on a sofa, bed, or in a car
- ✅ Storage: when an unfolded desk has nowhere to live
- ✅ Check first: size, flex, and the late-pledge price plus shipping

Weigh the crowdfunding risk too. An independent analysis (PledgeRadar) rated the scale-up risk from the huge overfunding as Medium (55/100). **Raising 60 times the goal is proof of demand and a seed of delay.** If you already have a fixed desk, this board has little room to stand.
